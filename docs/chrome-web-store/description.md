Lead Generator is a browser extension that extracts contact and company information directly from LinkedIn Sales Navigator lead pages and public LinkedIn profile pages, so you can build a lead list without manually copying data field by field.

On a supported page, click the Lead Generator icon shown on the page to open the tool as a panel that floats over it — it stays open while you browse, instead of closing every time you click elsewhere. Drag the icon anywhere you like; its position is remembered.

**What it does**

- Extracts a person's name, surname, job position, LinkedIn link, and their company's name, country, and industry with a single click.
- Reads detailed company information — website, industry, location, size, and member count — directly from the company's LinkedIn page.
- Generates and validates a likely work email address based on the company's website domain, using a secure backend service — no credentials or accounts required.
- Optionally translates job titles into English.
- Optionally transliterates names written in non-Latin scripts (e.g. Cyrillic) into Latin characters.

**Organize and export leads**

- Save extracted leads to local storage with one click, and review or edit every field before saving.
- Filter the companies found on a page by location or size to focus on the leads that matter.
- Copy all saved leads to the clipboard in one step, ready to paste into a spreadsheet (tab-separated) or export as JSON.
- Configure how many leads can be stored locally, and clear saved leads at any time.

**Why install it**

Manually collecting lead information from LinkedIn is slow and error-prone. Lead Generator automates the repetitive parts — reading the page, formatting the data, checking email validity — while keeping you in control: nothing is extracted, saved, or copied without an explicit action on your part.

**Privacy**

The extension only runs on LinkedIn pages. All data is processed on your device; saved leads and preferences stay in local browser storage and are never sent to any server except the minimal, temporary requests needed for email validation, website checks, and translation, all of which are handled through a secure backend so no third-party API keys are ever exposed.
